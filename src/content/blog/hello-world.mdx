# Hello, World! 👋

Welcome to my portfolio and blog! I'm **Shoaib Hasan**, a Backend Engineer passionate about building Go-first distributed systems that handle real production scale.

## Who I Am

I specialize in event-driven architectures, authentication, billing, and observability. With 4+ years of experience building platforms at Placepoint, Gorillamove, and Poridhi, I've learned that great backend engineering is about understanding system behavior, trade-offs, and making complex systems reliable.

### What I Do

- **Backend Engineering**: Go-first microservices with REST and gRPC APIs
- **Event-Driven Systems**: Kafka, RabbitMQ, and AWS SQS for async processing
- **Auth & Billing**: JWT authentication, subscription billing, and payment platforms
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: AWS ECS, Lambda, Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform
- **Observability**: Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK for production monitoring

## My Journey

I'm based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh** 🇧🇩, and currently work as a Backend Engineer at **Placepoint**, where I've built an event-driven payment platform generating **$500K in revenue** while maintaining **99.9% uptime** for **30K+ users**.

My proudest achievements include the **Excellence Award for Technical Brilliance** at Placepoint (2025) and winning **6th place internationally** at the **International Blockchain Olympiad (IBCOL) 2023** in Amsterdam, Netherlands.
